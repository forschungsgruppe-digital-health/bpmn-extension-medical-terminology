# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We — the contributors and maintainers of this project (TU Dresden / Forschungsgruppe
Digital Health) — are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and professional
experience for everyone who takes part in our community, regardless of background or
personal characteristics.

This project adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1][cc]** as its Code of Conduct.
The expectations below summarize our standards; the linked canonical text is the
authoritative and complete version.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, considerate, and constructive in all interactions.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.
- Show empathy and kindness toward other community members.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate, intimidating, demeaning, or
otherwise harmful in a professional setting will not be tolerated. This includes conduct
that targets or excludes others on the basis of personal characteristics, as well as
sustained disruption of the community or the project's work. The full, enumerated standards
are set out in the linked Contributor Covenant text.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull
requests, and any other project communication channels — and also applies when an
individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.
